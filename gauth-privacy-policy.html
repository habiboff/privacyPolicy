Official Application Privacy Policy

1. Zero Data Collection Policy

At Mac Authenticator, we prioritize your security and privacy above all else:

  • We DO NOT COLLECT, store, track, or transmit any personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, or telemetry.
  • No 2FA/TOTP accounts, secret keys, or account labels created or imported into the application are ever transmitted to external servers or third parties.

2. Local Storage & Hardware Security

All sensitive information is securely handled exclusively on your local device:

  • Apple Keychain Services: All TOTP secret keys and your Master Passcode are stored exclusively within your local macOS Keychain using hardware-backed, industry-standard encryption.
  • Local SQLite Database: Account metadata (names, issuers, sort orders) is stored locally on your device via SwiftData / SQLite.
  • Biometric Authentication (Touch ID): Touch ID verification is processed directly by Apple's Secure Enclave hardware. Mac Authenticator never accesses your raw fingerprint data.

3. 100% Offline Operation

Mac Authenticator is engineered to operate completely offline. Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP - RFC 6238) are calculated locally using your Mac's internal clock without requiring any internet connection.

4. System Permissions (User-Selected Files)

The application only accesses file directories when you explicitly use NSOpenPanel (File Picker) to select a QR code image file. The selected image is processed entirely in-memory and is never exported or shared outside your device.

5. Right to Modify Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update, amend, or modify this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice. Any updates will take effect immediately upon publication on this page. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Mac Authenticator does not collect any personal data, it complies with all international children's online privacy protection regulations.
