AsFar Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

In short

AsFar collects no personal data, shares nothing and sells nothing. Your photos never leave your device.

About AsFar

AsFar is a game that turns your own photos into jigsaw puzzles. We care about your privacy, and this policy explains which information the app uses and how.

Photos and camera

  • To choose a photo, AsFar uses the iOS photo picker. The app only receives the photo you pick and cannot see the rest of your photo library.
  • AsFar uses the camera only when you tap “Take photo”, and only with your permission.
  • The photo you pick or take is used solely to create your puzzle and is stored only on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Your puzzles (photo, progress and time) and your settings (language, piece count, difficulty and sound) are stored only on your device, inside the app’s own storage.
  • This information may be included in your device backups, including iCloud Backup, through Apple’s backup system. These backups are subject to Apple’s privacy terms, and we have no access to them.
  • Deleting the app removes this information from your device. You can also delete any puzzle from within the app.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for an account, name, email address, location or contacts.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not share or sell any information to third parties.

Children’s privacy

AsFar is suitable for players of all ages. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we collect no information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
